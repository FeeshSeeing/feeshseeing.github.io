# 🐟 Feesh Seeing 
### *A cozy visual guide for your FFXIV aquarium planning.*

[**Explore the Fish Library**](https://feeshseeing.github.io/)

---

## ✨ Welcome, Eorzean Interior Designer!
Have you ever spent hours decorating your house, only to realize that the fish you just bought doesn't quite match the vibe? **Feesh Seeing** is a hobby project born out of a love for FFXIV housing and the many beautiful (and sometimes goofy) creatures that inhabit our waters.

This is a visual-first guide designed to help you preview aquarium fish before you commit those precious Gil or Scrips.

---

## 🌿 Features
* **Visual Previews:** Get a feel for the colors and details of each fish to see how they fit your room's aesthetic.
* **Easy Filtering:** Browse by size (Small to XL) or search for your favorites.
* **Quick Links:** Find info on where to catch them or check current Market Board prices.
* **Mobile Friendly:** Plan your next aquarium build while lounging in your favorite chair or waiting in a DPS queue.

> [!NOTE]  
> **A quick heads-up:** The fish images are resized for display purposes to give you a clear look at their designs. They are not shown at their exact 1:1 in-game scale.

## 🥐 How to Contribute
Want to help keep the library up to date? 
1.  **Report a Bug:** Open an [issue](https://github.com/feeshseeing/feeshseeing.github.io/issues) if a fish is missing its image or something looks wonky.
2.  **Submit Images:** If you have a high-quality screenshot of a missing fish in an aquarium, feel free to reach out!
3. **Feedback:** Suggestions for new features are always welcome! As this is a hobby project, I’ll do my best to consider them as my time and schedule allow.
